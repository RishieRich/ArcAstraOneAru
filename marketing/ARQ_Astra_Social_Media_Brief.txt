SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM (why this matters to people)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Millions of Indian small and medium businesses run their accounts on
Tally. Tally is powerful, but it's built for accountants — not for a
busy owner who just wants one answer: "Who owes me money right now,
and who's overdue?"

Today that means:
  - Scrolling through Tally reports that only the accountant reads
  - Manually building Excel sheets to track outstanding payments
  - Calling customers to ask "when will you pay?" without real data
  - No easy way to see the business in Hindi/Gujarati/Marathi, not
    just English

ARQ Astra removes all of that friction.

SECTION 4 — WHAT ARQ ASTRA DOES (plain English, no jargon)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Connects to Tally automatically
     A small app sits next to Tally on the owner's computer and reads
     (never changes) receivables data — who owes what, and since when.

  2. Shows it on a clean online dashboard
     Open it from a phone or laptop, anywhere — no need to be at the
     shop's computer or open Tally itself.

  3. Speaks the owner's language
     Available in English, Hinglish, Gujarati and Marathi. Switch
     languages any time.

  4. Has an AI copilot built in
     Owners can literally type a question like "which customer owes
     the most this month?" and get a straight answer — no formulas,
     no reports to build.

  5. Also works without Tally
     Businesses can upload an Excel or CSV file of their sales,
     purchases or expenses instead, and still get the same dashboard.

  6. Is safe by design
     The connector only reads data — it can never write to or change
     anything inside Tally. Each business's data is kept completely
     separate from every other business.
